For the first time, a different consensus engine is being integrated into the Cosmos SDK without forking or rewriting the SDK itself — enabling HotStuff to run in place of CometBFT through direct compatibility work. And while the integration is still a work in progress, it will push the boundaries of what Cosmos chains can do — especially for applications that need faster finality, lower latency, and no risk of reorgs. This article explains what’s happening, why it matters, and what it unlocks — for Cosmos, Meter, and beyond.
