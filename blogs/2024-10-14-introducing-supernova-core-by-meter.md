Introducing Supernova Core: Elevating the Cosmos SDK Experience For a long time, the Cosmos SDK has been the go-to solution for developers looking to create sovereign, application-specific blockchains that support interoperability and modularity. It offers a comprehensive toolkit, providing all the necessary components and frameworks to build, customize, and launch a blockchain from the ground up — eliminating the need to reinvent core blockchain infrastructure. While the Cosmos SDK excels in many areas, it also presents challenges, particularly at its core consensus engine.
